**RPM vs distance: slope −0.47 RPM/in, i.e. −15 RPM across the whole 32 in
span.** Wrong sign (further should need MORE), and tiny.

**Mean band width 95 RPM. Total spread of balance points 108 RPM.** The band is
as wide as the entire variation, so the variation is noise. A single RPM near
**1280** sits inside every one of these bands.

So `ShotTable` stays empty for now, and that is the correct answer rather than a
missing one. Set one RPM and move on.

**Why the width number is trustworthy even though the distances may not be:**
width is measured at a FIXED position from the balls shot there, so it does not
depend on the pose at all. The distances could be off (a rear wheel was dead,
the robot was partly pushed, and `cam trim` was never checked) and "the band is
wide" would still hold. That is why the conclusion rests on width, not slope.

## The two leads worth chasing

**1. Extend the RANGE, don't refine inside it.** 20–52 in is close. The camera
reads tags at 30–60 in and matches are shot from further out. If a distance term
exists it will show up between 55 and 80 in, not between 20 and 52. Next session:
3–4 spots at 55 / 65 / 75 in, turret near 0 (back to the hive).

**2. Extreme turret angles may genuinely shoot worse.** The −96.6° spot scored
**43%**, far the worst in the set, and the two extremes differ by 88 RPM
(+92° → 1305, −96.6° → 1217) which is the shape of a `b·sin(turret)` term with
b ≈ 44. But those two spots are at 20 in and 45 in, so angle and distance are
confounded and it is two points. **To separate them: 3–4 spots at the SAME
distance with the turret at −90 / −45 / 0 / +45 / +90.** That is the phase-2
sweep, and it is now the higher-value half.

## Housekeeping found while doing this

- The log is `/sdcard/shotmap/`, **not** `/sdcard/FIRST/shotmap/` —
  `AppUtil.ROOT_FOLDER` is `/sdcard`. The op-mode comment said FIRST/ and cost a
  search. Fixed; the on-screen path was always right.
- It lives on the **Control Hub**, not the Driver Station, and Android does not
  expose `/sdcard` as mass storage. `adb` over wifi (`adb connect
  192.168.43.1:5555`) needs no cable at all.
- One spot (`#8`, 39.6 in) has `rpm_bounded = false` — B was pressed before the
  band closed, so its 1244 is an extrapolation. Excluded above.
