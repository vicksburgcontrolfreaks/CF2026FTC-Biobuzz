## START HERE: one thing is live and has never run on a robot

**`TurretDirector.USE_ODOMETRY_AIM = true` for 6494.** `DriveTeleOp`,
`RouteAuto` and `PollenShotTune` now aim the turret **from the pose**, with the
camera trimming the angle, instead of camera-only. Shane turned it on at the end
of the session; **it has not been driven yet.**

Before trusting it in practice, on the robot:

1. **INIT `DriveTeleOp` and read the `Aim by` line.** It should say
   `odometry + camera trim` (or `odometry (no camera fix yet)` until a tag is
   seen). If it says anything starting with `camera`, odometry aiming did not
   engage and the rest of the line says why — most likely the turret geometry or
   the hive opening reading NaN.
2. **Watch the distance on that same line.** It should be a believable number of
   inches to the hive and should change as you drive. This is new — camera-only
   aiming never produced a distance at all.
3. **Watch the trim.** A couple of degrees is the camera correcting pose drift,
   which is the whole point. Tens of degrees, or a trim that keeps growing,
   means something is wrong — suspect the pose.
4. **Check the turret actually tracks while driving**, rather than stepping and
   pausing the way camera-only aiming did.

### How to back out, in order of how much you keep

| Change | Effect |
|---|---|
| `TurretDirector.USE_CAMERA_TRIM = false` | keep odometry aiming, ignore the camera |
| `TurretDirector.USE_ODOMETRY_AIM = false` | back to camera-only, i.e. the match-proven behaviour |
| `AimLead.LEAD_SEC = 0` | keep everything, drop the turret lead |

All three live in **`RobotProfile`**, per robot, installed at op-mode init.
5618 is `false` and should stay there until its `TURRET_OFFSET_X/Y` are
measured — the director falls back to the camera while they are NaN and says so.

## What changed today

### The hive model is now measured, not invented

Four made-up numbers became measurements, each carrying its own provenance in the
code. `CELL_ARM` 9.4 → **14.0**, `CELL_DROP` 10.0 → **1.44**, `HIVE_SWING_DEG`
40 → **60**, `RED_HIVE_X` 60.0 → **59.6**, and the hive opening went from three
NaNs to **x 59.6, y 55, z 59**.

`CELL_DROP` alone was wrong by 11 inches, which would have landed whole in every
tag-derived robot position.

**The rule that came out of it, and it is in the code:** `HiveTags` (tags, on the
cell's back face) and `HiveGeometry` (the opening, on the front face) are 12 in.
apart through the cell, and **neither may be computed from the other**. Three
attempts, three wrong numbers. Measure instead. See
`docs/hive-opening-measure.md`.

### Turret and camera aiming are now one thing

`TurretDirector` owns turret aiming for all three op modes. Odometry drives the
turret continuously; the camera corrects the angle via a filtered **trim** that
keeps working after the tag leaves view. Full reasoning in the class comment.

`TurretAim.bearingOf()` now aims at the **centre of the cell** instead of at
whichever tags are in frame. Before this the aim point moved up to 6.5 in.
depending on how many tags were visible, silently, and 1–2 tags is the normal
case. `CENTRE_ON_CELL` reverts it.

### Per-robot split

`RobotProfile` holds everything that differs between 5618 and 6494 — turret
offsets, camera mount, turret lead, the odometry-aim switch — and installs it at
init. Before this, 6494's measurements were in shared constants and 5618 used
them silently.

### Three turret questions measured and closed

- **kP is not the lever.** Tracking lag passes 40 deg on a spin, and
  `MAX_POWER / kP` = 40 deg is where the turret saturates. The servo does about
  **420 deg/s at full power, ~330 at `MAX_POWER` 0.80**, derived from the lead
  Shane tuned. A fast chassis spin is at or past that, so the turret cannot
  track one. Levers are `MAX_POWER` or not spinning that fast.
- **The turret settles cleanly** — 0 or 1 overshoots, worst 1.29 deg. So the
  residual aim wander is **the pose**, not the controller. `TOLERANCE_DEG` could
  go to about 1.5 but not lower, and it is not worth the time.
- **The turret lead wants 0.12 s on 6494.** Tuned on the robot; past 0.15 the
  aim visibly leads into turns.

2. **6494's phantom sideways odometry drift** (`docs/TODO.md` §0). Now the
   dominant error in everything that aims. Deliberately deferred: the robot is
   being changed and odometry needs retuning after.
3. **Shooter tuning on the new build.** Not blocked any more —
   `TurretDirector.getDistance()` gives `ShotTable` the distance it needs.
4. **Promote the lead into `Turret` as a real `kFF` term** so it is part of the
   controller rather than the aiming layer. Needs reworking the `TOLERANCE_DEG`
   early return (the loop must not stop while the target is still moving) and
   the `MIN_POWER` boost, inside the class `DriveTeleOp` aims with in matches —
   which is why it was not done this way first.
5. **Pose correction proper** — `TagLocalizer` → `VisionPoseFilter` → the pose.
   Both exist and are laptop-tested; `TagLocalizer` is wired into nothing. It
   would fix *position* error and therefore range, which the angle trim cannot.
   Needs `CAMERA_LEFT` and `CAMERA_YAW_DEG` measured — both still assumed 0.
6. **Tag visibility is NOT field-wide.** From the shooting positions the camera
   reliably sees 1–4 tags, but with the hive facing up it sees nothing from the
   far side or much of the blue side. Nothing may assume tags during a
   full-field traverse.
7. **Turret INIT homing legality** — the 150 deg sweep is still unconfirmed
   inside the 18 in. starting cube. Settle it when the turret gets flipped.
8. **Flipping the turret** to move the blind arc off the front. With
   `TURRET_ZERO_DEG = 0` and ±90 limits, the rear half of the robot is
   unreachable, and every blind-arc crossing costs tens of degrees of recovery.

## Instruments built today, so nobody rebuilds them

All in `Turret Odometry Aim Test` unless noted. All work **without Panels**.

| Control / readout | Does |
|---|---|
| **D-pad L/R** | step `AimLead.LEAD_SEC` by 0.05; peak resets each step |
| **LB** | swap this robot's turret gains ↔ baseline 0.010 / 0.55 |
| **RB** | reset the peak |
| `Aim error` | **tracking lag** (command vs actual) |
| `Total off target` | the whole miss, including anything out of reach |
| `Aim error PEAK` | lag peak, and **why it is paused** when it is |
| `Power-saturated past` | `MAX_POWER / kP`; flags when kP has stopped mattering |
| `Profile` | which robot's numbers are installed |

`Turret Test` also gained a **settle measurement**: HOLD mode, 2 deg bumper
nudge, then `Overshoots since nudge` and `Min step (deg/loop at MIN_POWER)`.

> **The lag peak lied three times before it was right** — once reporting the
> blind arc, once reporting its own 45 deg cap, once reporting the recovery out
> of the blind arc. If a measurement here ever reads suspiciously round or
> suspiciously constant, check what it is excluding before believing it.
